# Surface brief: Garage (homepage)

Mode: Operate. Mobile-first; desktop is the expanded version of the same surface.

## Scope and task
The owner opens "My Garage" to see every vehicle at once and know which one needs them. Frequent, quick, often one-handed, outdoors. One tap opens a vehicle.

## Content on the surface
Per vehicle: photo (or its paint-colour stage), make and model, plate, current odometer, the Pit Board sentence, and an attention state. Above the vehicles, only when something needs action: one summary line that expands to the cross-vehicle list.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The Garage is a row of cars, each wearing its own paint. It refuses the pale hairline list of the first build and the category default of a header of KPI tiles; colour and scale carry the page, and each vehicle is a rounded card the owner recognises at a glance by its colour.
OWN-WORLD: "The Paint Shop". Warm paper ground (#F2EEE6), white cards (radius 24), ink #17140F, saffron-yellow brand #FFC53D for actions and selection, status as vivid washes (green, amber, red) with words. Each vehicle contributes a large flat disc of its own paint over a pale tint of the same paint. Bricolage Grotesque for headings (the make light, the model bold), Geist for body, Barlow Semi Condensed for figures and plates. Flat colour only: no gradients, no glow. Dark mode: warm charcoal ground, lifted cards, the same yellow.
STORY: The owner sees four coloured cards, recognises their own vehicles by colour before reading, reads one plain sentence per vehicle, and opens the one that matters.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone, 390 wide. "My Garage" in the display face with the account control, then a one-line "what needs you" card, then the first vehicle card: a 168px stage (paint disc top-right, pale paint tint, the make in light weight over the model in bold), the plate chip and odometer figure, and a colour-washed Pit Board strip. The next card peeks in below. The docked yellow Add button sits bottom-right. Desktop: sidebar plus a grid of the same cards.
FORM: Pinned by the owner through reference images (RentalX card and dark panel, per-vehicle colour discs); no direction roll was run.
SIGNATURE MOVE: The paint disc: each vehicle's own colour as a large flat shape behind its name, repeated on its vehicle page.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
